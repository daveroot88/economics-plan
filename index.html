Online quizzes

Due Tuesday nights by 9:00pm Mountain Time. Retake as many times as you want before the deadline — only the highest score counts, and each attempt draws new questions. You may consult notes and the textbook. Your lowest six quiz scores are dropped.

Writing assignments

Eight total; your lowest two are dropped. Each is a 2-page (double-spaced, 12pt) analysis of an economics podcast. The first line = podcast title and source only. No quotations. Submit via Learning Suite by the 9:00pm deadline — late or emailed assignments are not accepted, and everything runs through Turnitin.

Exams

All taken in the Testing Center: closed-book, closed-note, multiple choice. Go within the listed window — pick one day. No make-up or early exams. One midterm can be dropped with its weight moved to the final. Notify the instructor in the first week of any exam-date conflicts.
